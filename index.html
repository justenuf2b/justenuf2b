Why this matters

When speed outpaced people’s sense of safety

Somewhere between the rush to automate and the need to perform, people started feeling left behind.

Leaders never set out to create fear. But after so many reorganizations and layoffs, even good intentions can sound like noise.

The real opportunity now isn’t more technology. It’s rebuilding trust.
